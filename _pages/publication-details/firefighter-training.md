## Research overview

This manuscript focuses on evaluating visual–thermal integration in mixed-reality firefighter training, with situational awareness and decision performance as the central concerns.

## Visual–thermal integration for training

How can visual and thermal information support awareness and decisions in a simulated fire?

## My role in this research program

Built thermal haptic hardware and developed the FDS data pipeline that connected simulated fire conditions with sensory feedback in XR.

## Connection to my broader work

The technical challenge of delivering realistic cues led to questions about simpler devices and the way users interpret feedback.
