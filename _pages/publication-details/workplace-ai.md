## Research question

How can a conversational interface support access to workplace information?

## Approach

This conference work explores an LLM-based chatbot intended to support worker efficiency and the prevention of safety incidents.

It forms an early part of my work on interfaces that connect people to information relevant to an action or decision.

## My contribution

Coauthored an LLM-based chatbot system for worker efficiency and safety.

## Connection to my broader work

The theme carries into later work on missing evidence and spatial AI: how information is presented affects how people can use it.
