## Research question

How can small IMU modules be evaluated using an accessible testbed?

## Approach

An inertial measurement unit combines sensors used to describe motion. Before interpreting data from such a device, the behavior of the sensor itself needs to be evaluated.

This conference paper presents a low-cost evaluation testbed using a smartphone. It is listed in ICCE-Asia 2025.

## My contribution

Coauthored work on a low-cost quantitative evaluation testbed for small IMU sensor modules.

## Connection to my broader work

Reliable interpretation begins with understanding the measurement that supports it.
