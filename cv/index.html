Research interests

Augmenting how we perceive the world.

My research investigates human perception and sensemaking, drawing on HCI, XR/haptics, and behavioral research.

I aim to understand the cognitive and neural mechanisms underlying perception and decision-making, and translate these insights into systems that augment human cognition.
