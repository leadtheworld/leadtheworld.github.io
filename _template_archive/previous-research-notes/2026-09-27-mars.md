## The question

How can wearable haptic interfaces make a remote world tangible?

## The approach

This line of work explores encountered force and thermal feedback in a Mars exploration setting. The interface connects physical sensation with a virtual environment.

The related publications span a multimodal simulation, a science exhibit proposal, a wrist-worn articulated robot, and a wearable thermal-kinesthetic interface.

## My contribution

Research on thermal haptic interfaces and embodied XR experiences with the Interaction Independence Study group.

## A connected question

The same interest in physically grounded feedback also connects to firefighter training, where temperature can carry information about a situation.
