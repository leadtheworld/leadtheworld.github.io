## The question

How can audio sensing make everyday information more accessible?

## The approach

This research direction investigates beamforming-based audio sensing to enhance perception of everyday information. Beamforming uses information across microphones to emphasize signals from particular directions.

The application question concerns which signals should be brought to a person’s attention, and how sensing can become useful information.

## My contribution

Investigating beamforming-based audio sensing at KAIST WIT Lab.

## A connected question

The broader question links sensing to interpretation: detecting a signal does not by itself explain its meaning to a person.
