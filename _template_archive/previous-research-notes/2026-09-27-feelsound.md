## The question

How can sound-derived signals shape a haptic experience?

## The approach

FeelSound explores the relationship between audio-derived signals and tactile interaction. The work uses granular synthesis to construct feedback for interactive tasks.

A key research question is how properties of the generated signal relate to what a person can distinguish and how they describe the resulting experience.

## My contribution

Developing audio-driven granular synthesis for high-DOF vibrotactile feedback at KAIST WIT Lab.

## A connected question

This project connects signal design with perceptual evaluation: changing an input only matters through the experience it produces.
