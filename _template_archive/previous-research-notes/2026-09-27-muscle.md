## The question

How can a measurement system make muscle assessment more accessible?

## The approach

At the Smart Biomedical Lab, I worked on a muscle function assessment device combining a load cell and an inertial measurement unit. The system communicates using Bluetooth Low Energy.

The project included a mobile app for real-time sensing and accessible interaction for older adults, in collaboration with Korea University Medicine and KAIST.

## My contribution

Developed a load cell and IMU device with BLE, and built a mobile app for real-time sensing and accessible interaction for older adults.

## A connected question

The interface is part of the measurement system: information needs to be accessible to the person who uses it.
