## The question

How can information and AI support remain grounded in a spatial environment?

## The approach

This manuscript brings together a VR digital twin, building information modeling, and role-separated LLM agent services. Its application is HVAC operation and maintenance.

The spatial setting is central to the research question: the information a person needs is connected to where a system or component exists within a building.

## My contribution

Coauthored a VR-based digital twin framework for building operation and maintenance.

## A connected question

This work connects interactive environments with access to information and decision support.
