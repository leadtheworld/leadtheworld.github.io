## The question

How can people learn from a loss when emotion and vague lessons make reflection difficult?

## The approach

FacingLoss supports chronological reconstruction, reflection from multiple perspectives, and the articulation of personal investment principles. AI offers editable suggestions while the user retains control over interpretation.

The research includes interviews with 12 investors and an exploratory study with 12 participants. Findings are preliminary: the study has no control group and a one-week follow-up. The manuscript is under review.

## My contribution

First-authored work on a system for reconstructing loss experiences and forming personal investment principles.

## A connected question

The project motivates a continuing question about how interpretations change. Supporting reflection and explaining its underlying cognitive processes are complementary research goals.
