## The question

Can showing missing evidence help people examine whether they have enough information to decide?

## The approach

Investment interfaces commonly present information that is already available. This project focuses attention on the evidence that is absent and the questions still left unanswered.

The work is published in the CHI 2026 Extended Abstracts. It explores an interface for supporting informed investment decisions through the visualization of missing evidence.
