## The question

How can visual and thermal information support awareness and decisions in a simulated fire?

## The approach

Firefighter training brings perception and action close together: a cue matters because of what a person does with it. This work examines visual and thermal integration in mixed reality.

The two manuscripts in this area address situational awareness, decision performance, sensemaking, and cognitive load. Both are listed as under review in my CV.

## My contribution

Physics-based fire visualization and thermal interface prototyping within a collaborative firefighter-training research effort.

## A connected question

This work connects the design of a sensory cue with the larger question of how someone interprets that cue in context.
